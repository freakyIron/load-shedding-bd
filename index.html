Why this estimate

← lowers riskraises risk →

This checker uses a 12-input version of the research model. On 2025–2026 data it never saw, it ranked risky days better than the rule "tomorrow equals today" (PR-AUC vs ) and caught of load-shedding days, but it raises more false alarms, so its everyday accuracy () is below that rule's (). Use it as a risk signal, not a guarantee.

Data

  • PGCB hourly grid records, 2015–2026, cross-checked against a second release (99.9% of hours match)
  • BPDB daily reports with supply limits, Dhaka temperature and nine distribution zones, to August 2026

Method

  • Time-ordered split: train Jul 2022–Jun 2024, validate H2 2024, test Jan 2025–Mar 2026
  • Logistic regression, random forest, XGBoost and LightGBM tuned with Optuna; explained with SHAP

Limits

  • Temperature is Dhaka only, and observed rather than forecast
  • SHAP shows what the model relies on, not proven causes
  • Official figures may understate the shedding people experience
